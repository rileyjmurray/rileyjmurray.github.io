In 2016 I built a scheduling system for the [San Francisco Fire Department's](https://sf-fire.org/) ambulance fleet. It started as a UC Berkeley capstone and I subsequently refined it for performance, testability, and ease of adoption. The code is publicly available, and I remain happy to help adapt it for other municipalities — get in touch.
