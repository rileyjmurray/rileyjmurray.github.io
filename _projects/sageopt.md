[sageopt](https://github.com/rileyjmurray/sageopt) implements the SAGE (Sums of AM/GM Exponentials) hierarchy for signomial and polynomial nonnegativity certification, as well as the methods I developed in [my PhD thesis](https://thesis.library.caltech.edu/14092/). It grew out of my graduate research and is the reference implementation for several papers in this area.

Documentation: <https://rileyjmurray.github.io/sageopt/>.
