I've been a core developer of [CVXPY](https://www.cvxpy.org/) — the Python-embedded modeling language for convex optimization — since 2018, and I sit on its steering committee. CVXPY is used widely in research, teaching, and industry; my contributions have spanned conic reformulations, solver interfaces, and language design.
