While I served as president of UC Berkeley's [Alpha Pi Mu](https://www.alphapimu.com/) chapter, I led a research project surveying Industrial Engineering and Operations Research curricula across U.S. universities. The compiled findings were intended for both faculty designing programs and prospective students choosing one.
